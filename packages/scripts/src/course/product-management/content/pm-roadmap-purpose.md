In January, Roost's roadmap was a slide with five features and a month beside each: owner chat in April, photo reviews in May, instalments in June. Owner partnerships showed it to 60 hostel owners, who liked "owner chat, April" best. In March the team scored chat against the season goal, found it fourth of five, and dropped it for the season. Nobody updated the slide the owners had. In April, 60 owners asked where chat was.

The plan changed for a good reason, and the roadmap still failed: the team read it as a guess, the owners as a promise.

## Blueprint or compass

The course's instructor puts it in one line: roadmaps are not just blueprints, they are compasses, and they evolve with user feedback, technical feasibility and business priority. A blueprint specifies what gets built, and departing from it is a mistake. A compass gives a direction and leaves the path open.

The metaphor points at the job. A roadmap carries direction and order to people who are not in the room when decisions are made: engineers, the founder, sales, partners, sometimes customers. That makes it a communication tool first, and it means what its reader takes it to mean. A dated feature list will be read as a promise, whatever the footnote says.

## Features or outcomes

There are two formats. A **feature roadmap** lists outputs on a timeline. An **outcome roadmap** lists problems to solve or results to move, each tied to an objective, with solutions left open until discovery settles them. The same slice of Roost's season, both ways:

```text
Feature roadmap
  April    Owner chat
  May      Photo reviews
  June     Instalments

Outcome roadmap
  Objective   Families book a room they have not seen
  Problem     Parents will not pay until someone has
              seen the room
  Measure     Request-to-booking, 50% to 60%
  Current bet Parent page: visit date, dated photos,
              charges, pay
  Candidates  Availability confirmation, map pin fix
```

The outcome version still names solutions, but as bets under a problem, replaceable when the evidence says so, with a measure that tells every reader whether the bet worked.

Marty Cagan made the case against the feature format in "The Inconvenient Truth About Product" (SVPG, March 2013). His two truths: at least half of product ideas will not work, and the ones that do usually take several iterations before they deliver the value the business expected. A dated feature list commits to both unknowns before either is known. In "The Alternative to Roadmaps" (September 2015) he proposed giving teams the product vision and business objectives, often as OKRs, and letting them find the solutions.

## What a roadmap promises

A roadmap should promise:

- the problems the team is working on, and the objective each one serves
- the order it will take them in, and why
- that it will change, and how changes will be announced

It must not promise a solution, or a date for one, before discovery has tested it.

The outcome format breaks in two ways. First, "improve trust" without a metric and a baseline is a slogan, not an outcome. Second, when the solution is already known, naming it is honest. The map pin bug has a found cause and a known fix, and "fix the map pin" says more than "listings appear where they are". Outcome framing is a tool for uncertainty, not a ritual. When a date is genuinely needed is this section's last lesson.

## Predict, then verify

Owner partnerships wants a slide for an owner meetup of about 200 owners, with "Owner chat, July" on it: 25 large hostels say they will buy Featured at ₹6,000 a month if chat is coming, which is 25 x ₹6,000 = ₹150,000 a month. Chat scored fourth of five against the season goal, at 50% confidence, for three person-months of work. What goes on the slide?

Answer: the problem, not the feature. The slide says "Owners answer booking requests faster", with today's number (40% of requests wait more than a day for a reply) and its place in the order, behind the parent page and availability. The 25 hostels hear the same truth: the problem is on the roadmap, the solution is not chosen, and chat is one candidate. A dated chat item bets the goodwill behind ₹150,000 a month on a coin flip, and January showed how that bet ends. The principle: a roadmap commits to problems and their order; a solution earns a date only after discovery shows it works.
